# Player guide

## Your first investigation

1. Create a character using one of the 18 presets, or choose Custom and select 12 skills.
2. Distribute the 12 starting attribute points in the Character screen.
3. Locate **Civic Liaison** near City Hall, using the atlas/transit if you prefer, and press **E** nearby.
4. Ask about the **Bluewater File** and follow the objective in your journal (**J**).
5. Travel to the fictional service complex marked in your atlas, enter with **E** and investigate lockers. Some require lockpicking.
6. Use the indoor atlas's floor selector and physical stairs to navigate. Optional service panels may reveal shortcuts.
7. Return to Civic Liaison for the pumping-station lead and find both records.
8. Decide between disclosure through **Neighbourhood Aid** and a confidential settlement via **Civic Liaison**.
9. Continue with career jobs, training, purchases and exploration after the main case.

## Movement and combat

WASD and the mouse move and look. Shift sprints, Ctrl sneaks, Space jumps. Hold E while pushing against a wall to climb. Left mouse uses your weapon/tool; right mouse uses a field device selected with keys 1–8. **B** cycles owned walking, bicycle and van movement modes.

## Character progression

The game uses eight attributes and 35 skills. Three Primary, three Major and six Minor skills define a profession; practice advances skills, and rest/training support progression. The three Primary skills, two best Major skills and best Minor skill count toward character level. Attribute points are awarded on level-up. The game uses its own simplified modern rules, not a recreation of another RPG's exact mechanics.

## Work, equipment and the city

Six fictional organizations provide jobs, reputation, ranks and payments. Shops sell equipment and supplies; workshops repair and upgrade gear. Banks and property records support financial progression. Field devices use battery capacity for healing, protection, concealment, surveying and other effects.

The street atlas is an offline tile map with fictional game markers. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and use transit stops when available. Generated interiors have separate floor maps. The visual geography uses approximate OpenStreetMap-derived coordinates. Interior plans, people and activities are fictional.

## Saving and menus

**F5** saves and **F9** loads one local save slot. Godot manages the save file outside the game folder. The game clock pauses in menus but advances during resting, training and travel. Quest deadlines and debts may continue when time skips.

**Tab** Character · **I** Inventory · **J** Journal · **M** Atlas · **F** Factions · **T** Devices · **R** Rest · **Esc** Pause.

For Windows installation and save paths see the player ZIP's README, and for caveats see [Known Limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
